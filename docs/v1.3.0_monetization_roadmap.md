# v1.3.0 Roadmap — Monetization and Intelligence Product Layer

This roadmap opens the monetization and intelligence-product layer for World Cup Market Intelligence.

v1.0.0 created the public MVP.

v1.1.0 added data quality, freshness and trust.

v1.2.0 made the project portfolio-ready.

v1.3.0 turns the project into a monetizable research product.

The project remains research-only.

It is not betting advice.

It is not investment advice.

It is not a prediction engine.

Powered by Mayior Capital.

## Strategic shift

The original idea was broader:

- general market intelligence
- different market segments
- event-driven opportunities
- narrative and catalyst tracking
- probability movement
- structured research workflows

The first completed use case became:

- FIFA World Cup 2026 prediction-market intelligence

This is useful because the World Cup is a clean event-based test case.

It has:

- teams
- groups
- matches
- market prices
- probability movement
- liquidity
- catalysts
- news flow
- public interest
- clear timeline

The strategic shift is:

World Cup Market Intelligence becomes the first monetizable vertical of a broader Mayior Event Intelligence engine.

## Free vs paid split

### Free layer

The free layer is used for credibility, SEO, public proof-of-work and trust.

Free assets:

- GitHub repo
- public landing page
- stable dashboard
- delayed trends dashboard
- sample reports
- public release notes
- documentation
- occasional public market notes

Purpose:

- demonstrate competence
- attract users
- build credibility
- create public proof-of-work

### Paid layer

The paid layer saves time and gives structured interpretation.

Paid assets may include:

- daily intelligence brief
- Telegram or Discord alerts
- weekly intelligence report
- custom watchlists
- team review priority
- catalyst summaries
- historical movement archive
- CSV exports
- premium dashboard view
- custom B2B reports

---

## Possible pricing

Early validation pricing:

- Free: public dashboards and sample reports
- Starter: 19 EUR/month
- Pro: 49 EUR/month
- Analyst: 99 EUR/month
- Custom report: 300-1500 EUR/report

Initial realistic target:

- 10 paying users at 19-49 EUR/month
- 1-3 custom reports
- validate demand before building complex infrastructure

The goal is validation, not perfection.

Target users:

- prediction-market users

- market researchers
- sports-data enthusiasts
- content creators

- Discord or Telegram community owners

- people who want structured intelligence but not raw dashboards

Will 10-20 people pay 19-49 EUR/month for a useful daily event-market intelligence brief?

## Product principles

The product must be:

- transparent
- research-only
- useful even without trading
- dashboard-linked
- easy to read
- easy to produce daily
- legally conservative
- not dependent on overclaiming
- explainable
- reusable for other event categories

The product should avoid:

- hype
- guaranteed profit claims
- betting language
- official tournament branding
- complex login systems at the beginning
- premature API monetization
- overbuilding before demand

## Monetization path

The recommended path is:

1. Build daily brief generator
2. Publish one sample public brief
3. Create a Pro landing page
4. Share the public project
5. Ask for feedback from 30-50 targeted people
6. Offer early access manually
7. Validate willingness to pay
8. Only then build payment automation or private dashboard
