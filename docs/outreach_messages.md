## 15. Objection handling

### Objection: I do not bet

Response:

That is fine. The product is not designed as betting advice.

The useful part is market intelligence: what changed, where attention is moving, what catalysts may matter and which teams deserve manual review.

### Objection: Is this a prediction model?

Response:

No. It is not a prediction engine.

It is a transparent research workflow around market movement, liquidity, catalysts and freshness.

### Objection: Why would I pay?

Response:

The value is time saved and structured interpretation.

Instead of checking markets, dashboards, notes and movement manually, the brief summarizes what changed and what deserves review.

### Objection: Is the data reliable?

Response:

The project includes a trust layer with freshness and missing-output status.

Fresh data does not mean correct data, but it helps prevent overinterpreting stale dashboards.

### Objection: Is this affiliated with FIFA?

Response:

No. This is an independent research project around World Cup 2026 prediction-market data.

It is not official and not affiliated with FIFA.

## Final note

The goal of outreach is learning.

Do not overbuild before feedback.

The correct sequence is:

- share sample
- ask for feedback
- identify useful sections
- test repeated delivery
- test payment intent
- then build automation
