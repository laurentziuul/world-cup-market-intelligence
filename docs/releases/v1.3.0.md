This release opens the monetization and intelligence-product layer for World Cup Market Intelligence.

The core monetizable product is:

- World Cup Market Intelligence Daily Brief

- Mayior Event Intelligence

## Daily Brief generator

Added:

- scripts/generate_daily_brief.py

The generator creates:

- docs/briefs/latest.md
- docs/briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md

It reads available outputs from:

- data/processed/top_movers_latest.csv
- data/processed/probability_deltas_latest.csv
- data/processed/signal_summary_latest.csv
- data/processed/catalyst_matches_latest.csv
- data/processed/team_intelligence_latest.csv
- data/processed/dashboard_metadata_latest.json

The script is designed to continue working even when some optional generated files are missing.

Missing optional files are surfaced as warnings in the brief.

Run:

- python scripts/generate_daily_brief.py

## Monetization hypothesis

The first revenue hypothesis is:

Some users will pay for a daily World Cup prediction-market intelligence brief if it saves research time and highlights important market movement.

Potential early users:

- prediction-market users
- crypto users
- market researchers
- sports-data enthusiasts
- content creators
- newsletter writers
- Discord or Telegram community owners
- event-driven traders
- people who want structured intelligence but not raw dashboards

Early validation target:

- 10 to 20 people willing to pay 19 to 49 EUR/month

---

## Free vs paid split

Free layer:

- GitHub repository
- public dashboards
- sample brief
- delayed or public reports
- documentation
- open-source proof-of-work

Paid layer:

- daily intelligence brief
- alert delivery
- custom watchlists
- premium summaries
- weekly intelligence report
- custom B2B reports

The value is not raw data.

The value is:

- context
- prioritization
- interpretation
- consistency
- time saved
