# Public Demo Guide

This guide explains how to present World Cup Market Intelligence in approximately three minutes.

The goal is to show the project clearly as a public MVP and portfolio-ready proof-of-work project.

This is not betting advice.

This is not investment advice.

This is not a prediction engine.

Powered by Mayior Capital.

---

## Demo goal

In three minutes, the viewer should understand:

- what the project does
- why it exists
- what the public dashboards show
- what is stable
- what is experimental
- how catalyst notes work
- how team intelligence works
- how the trust layer improves interpretation
- why this is research-only

## 3-minute demo structure

### Minute 1 — Public landing page

Open:

- https://laurentziuul.github.io/world-cup-market-intelligence/

Explain:

- This is an open-source market intelligence project for FIFA World Cup 2026 prediction-market data.
- It combines dashboards, provider data, historical movement, catalyst notes, team intelligence and trust indicators.
- It is designed as a static public MVP.
- It is research-only, not betting advice.

Show the three public dashboard links:

- Stable dashboard
- Polymarket live dashboard
- Historical trends dashboard

---

### Minute 2 — Dashboard walkthrough

Open the stable dashboard:

- https://laurentziuul.github.io/world-cup-market-intelligence/dashboard/

Explain:

- This is the reproducible baseline.
- It uses manual CSV data.
- It is the safest and most stable layer.

Open the Polymarket dashboard:

- https://laurentziuul.github.io/world-cup-market-intelligence/polymarket-dashboard/

Explain:

- This is the experimental live-provider layer.
- It can show market probabilities, liquidity, volume and source links.
- It depends on external provider data, so it should not be overclaimed.

Open the trends dashboard:

- https://laurentziuul.github.io/world-cup-market-intelligence/trends-dashboard/

Explain:

- This is the main intelligence surface.
- It shows historical trends, top movers, signal summaries, catalyst matches and team intelligence.

---

### Minute 3 — Intelligence and trust layer

In the trends dashboard, highlight:

- Data freshness and trust status
- Team intelligence
- Top movers
- Signal summary
- Catalyst matches

Explain:

- Signal labels are transparent rule-based labels.
- Catalyst notes are manual explanations that may help interpret movement.
- Catalyst notes do not prove causality.
- Team intelligence aggregates signal and catalyst context by team.
- High review priority means manual research is needed, not that a team is a bet.
- The trust panel shows whether data is fresh, stale, missing or experimental.

End with:

- The project is a transparent, open-source, static dashboard MVP.
- It is suitable as a portfolio project and proof-of-work for market intelligence, AI-assisted building and public data products.
- It is not a betting system or prediction model.

## Short spoken version

World Cup Market Intelligence is an open-source public MVP for analyzing FIFA World Cup 2026 prediction-market data.

It has three public dashboard layers.

The stable dashboard uses manual CSV data and acts as the reproducible baseline.

The Polymarket dashboard is experimental and shows live-provider market data when available.

The trends dashboard is the main intelligence layer. It shows probability movement, top movers, signal classifications, catalyst matches and team-level intelligence.

The project also has a trust layer that shows freshness, missing outputs and dashboard status.

Everything is transparent, static-site friendly and research-only.

This is not betting advice or a prediction engine.
