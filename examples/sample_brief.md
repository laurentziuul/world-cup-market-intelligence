\## 5. Red-team



Why the current interpretation can be wrong:



1\. The dataset is manually entered and may not reflect live market prices.

2\. Volume and liquidity are not informative if they remain zero.

3\. A high implied probability may reflect reputation or public bias, not true edge.

4\. Without historical snapshots, we cannot distinguish a real probability move from a static baseline.

5\. Without multiple providers, we cannot compare prediction-market prices against sportsbook odds, exchange odds or other forecasting sources.
