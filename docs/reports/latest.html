What Changed in the Market?
What is a prediction market probability? On platforms like Polymarket, people trade contracts that pay out if a specific outcome happens. The price of those contracts reflects the collective market view — expressed as a percentage. When more people buy "Yes", the probability goes up. When more sell, it goes down. These numbers are not official odds or forecasts.

Section 3
Volume & Liquidity
Volume is the total amount of money that has been traded on a market. A rising volume means more people are actively participating — buying or selling contracts. Liquidity is the amount of money available to be traded right now. High liquidity means it is easier to enter or exit a position. Neither figure is a signal of who will win.
