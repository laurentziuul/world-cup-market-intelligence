## 8. Warnings and limitations

Important limitations:

- Prediction markets can be illiquid.
- Market prices can move because of narrative, not truth.
- Catalyst notes do not prove causality.
- Signal labels describe market movement, not future match outcomes.
- Fresh data can still be wrong or incomplete.
- Stale data can still be useful as historical context.
- Manual review is required before interpretation.

This brief should not be used as betting, investment or financial advice.
