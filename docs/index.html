Research-only disclaimer: this project is for education, open-source experimentation and market-intelligence research. It is not betting advice, investment advice, a prediction engine or a trading system.

What the MVP includes

  • Stable market dashboard based on manual CSV data.
  • Experimental live provider workflow for Polymarket-style data.
  • Historical trends comparing snapshots over time.
  • Signal classification for positive, negative and flat movement.
  • Catalyst notes for manual real-world context.
  • Narrative intelligence connecting market movement with possible explanations.
  • Team intelligence aggregating signal and catalyst context by team.
  • Safe local validation using scripts/validate_project.py.
  • Project health reporting using scripts/project_health.py.

How to read the project

Stable vs experimental

The manual CSV layer is the reproducible baseline. Live provider data, historical trends, catalyst matching and team intelligence are experimental.

Signals are not predictions

Signal labels describe market movement and liquidity context. They do not predict match results or recommend bets.

Catalysts do not prove causality

Catalyst notes provide transparent manual context. They may help explain a move, but they do not prove what caused it.
