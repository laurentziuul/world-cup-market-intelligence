This checklist verifies that the v1.3.0 monetization and intelligence-product layer is ready.

Status: draft checklist

## Release scope

v1.3.0 adds the first monetization layer to World Cup Market Intelligence.

It turns the project from a public portfolio MVP into a research-product prototype.

The core monetizable product is:

- World Cup Market Intelligence Daily Brief

The broader strategic direction is:

- Mayior Event Intelligence

World Cup Market Intelligence becomes the first event-intelligence vertical.

---

## Research-only positioning

The project must remain clearly research-only.

It is not:

- betting advice
- investment advice
- financial advice
- prediction engine
- trading bot
- guaranteed signal system
- black-box AI prediction system

The monetization layer sells:

- structure
- context
- prioritization
- market movement summaries
- catalyst watchlists
- freshness status
- manual review workflow
- time saved

It does not sell:

- betting tips
- guaranteed profit
- exact predictions
- trade recommendations
- financial recommendations

## Manual product validation checklist

Before building payment automation, validate manually:

- share sample brief with 30-50 targeted people
- ask if they understand the product
- ask if it saves research time
- ask what section is most useful
- ask if they would pay 19-49 EUR/month during the World Cup cycle
- ask what delivery channel they prefer
- collect objections
- improve the brief format before building SaaS infrastructure
