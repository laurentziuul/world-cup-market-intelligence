How to read this dashboard

Not familiar with prediction markets? Here is what each section means. These are research outputs, not betting tips.

Top positive mover
The team whose market-implied probability increased the most since the previous snapshot. It does not mean the team will win — only that the market moved upward.
Top negative mover
The team whose market-implied probability decreased the most since the previous snapshot.
pp — percentage points
The change in probability measured in percentage points, not percent. Example: 10.00% → 10.20% = +0.20 pp — not +20%.
Signal
A rule-based classification of market movement (e.g. sharp_move_up, low_volume_move). It is not a betting recommendation or prediction.
Catalyst
A possible manual context note for a market move (e.g. injury news, squad update). A catalyst match does not prove what caused the move — it is context, not causality.
Manual review priority
The team flagged for manual review first, based on signal and catalyst activity. It is not a pick or a recommendation of any kind.
Volume
How much has traded in this market. Higher volume can indicate more attention or conviction from market participants.
Liquidity
How much market depth is available. Higher liquidity means it is easier to trade near the current price.
Freshness status
Whether the underlying data is recent enough to trust. "ok" = fresh. "stale" = data is older than the stale threshold.
Mini glossary — click to expand
Probability Market-implied chance of an outcome (0%–100%). Not a prediction.
pp (percentage points) Absolute change in probability. 10% → 11% = +1 pp, not +10%.
Volume Total amount traded in the market.
Liquidity Available depth in the order book around the current price.
Signal label Rule-based movement classification. Not a buy/sell/bet signal.
Review priority Which team to check manually first. Not a recommendation.
Catalyst match A news event that may explain a market move. Correlation, not causation.
Stale data Data older than the configured freshness threshold. Treat with extra caution.
